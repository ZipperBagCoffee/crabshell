# Compaction Continuity

When context is compacted, reload the W document before continuing. Preserve these items verbatim:

- the user's original request and latest correction;
- required outcomes and non-goals;
- named authoritative references;
- allowed changes and forbidden side effects;
- observable success conditions;
- unresolved blocking unknowns;
- decisive observations already collected.

Compress narration and intermediate exploration, not the task contract or evidence. Reopen the newest W log and decisive references; do not infer completion from a prior worker/reviewer status.
